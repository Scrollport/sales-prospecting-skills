# Qualified Accounts to Contact 4.0.0 — internal verification

7 September 2026. Verified for bounded account research and professional-contact checking; not Customer-proven. This is not evidence of purchase intent or an outreach-ready campaign.

## Scope and result

A public payments-business brief sought up to three finance/operations contacts at UK restaurant groups. Three plausible groups were researched and one obvious country/business mismatch rejected from the discovery pool.

- One research lead had company/role evidence and a professional address verified `valid` after one unbilled provider failure and successful retry.
- One account required parent-company buyer resolution. Local-domain results were restaurant staff; the group finance address returned `accept_all` and remained incomplete.
- A third account's former finance leader had changed employers. That contact was rejected before address enrichment; current owner/trigger evidence remained incomplete.
- The first official-record query used a trading name and returned unrelated companies. First-party legal-name evidence resolved an exact active record.
- A database job title differed from the surfaced professional profile; the discrepancy was retained rather than upgraded into unsupported seniority.

**No account had directly validated unresolved payments pain.** Under a caller's mandatory demonstrated-pain criterion this yields zero qualified opportunities. Expansion, valid email and firmographics are not substitutes. CRM/customer-list reconciliation was unavailable; a public search with no hits is not proof of net-new status.

Sales-labelled settled cost: **$0.295100**, including incomplete/rejected work and recovery. Overall three-Skill cost was **$0.655100**, below the approved $5 ceiling, with no holds remaining. No outreach, CRM write or publication occurred.

## Generality and quality limits

The original-wording revision adopts the context/hard-fit/source discipline seen in [prospecting](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/5b2c0007766c6a1cf1d53fd8fc73e979e0821022/skills/prospecting/SKILL.md), from a repository above 10,000 stars at review. It adds a concrete Scrollport execution/evidence path, not a claim of superior output quality.

Unrelated static scenarios covered supplied US SaaS accounts, a manufacturer's mandatory pain gate and an independently buying local firm. UK official-record checks remain conditional; parent lookup is needed only where the buying entity requires it. No restaurant names, test roles or success counts are runtime rules. Cross-industry reliability and commercial usefulness require additional live briefs and external review.

An independent offline agent used a fictional supplied US SaaS list without expected answers. It excluded an existing customer and solved problem, kept funding separate from pain, and retained one research-qualified account with incomplete contact readiness. Resulting clarifications make fresh lookup conditional, distinguish qualification from contactability and disclose missing upstream receipts. This is a behaviour check, not additional live or customer proof.

A later unblinded offline comparison applied the reference Skill to the same frozen evidence. Both retained role and mailbox uncertainty; the comparison improved our report's provenance and separation of the research brief from the stricter launch-campaign qualification criterion, while the reference output missed stronger first-party expansion evidence. This does not establish general superiority or independent collection performance.
