## Limits

- Seven firms, not ten: the Find Leads run that fed this one found 7 ready
  contacts against 25 asked for and did not pass its own verification.
- The people routes, email lookups and email check were not exercised, because
  every contact came from Find Leads. The 12 October reverification remains the
  latest live check of those steps.
- One vertical and one region. Small accounting firms rarely publish dated
  news, so most verdicts were Not yet; yield elsewhere is not measured.
- The news search gives relative dates such as "1 month ago". The Contact now
  date was confirmed on the article's own page.
- A verified address is deliverable today. It does not prove consent, current
  role or buying authority.
