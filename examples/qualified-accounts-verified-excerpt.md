# Research My Accounts: sample from an earlier version

Status: abridged public synthetic example; verified 28 August 2026 with
Qualified Accounts to Contact, the earlier version of Research My Accounts; not
Customer-proven.

That version also found the companies itself and did not look for dated
reasons to reach out. Research My Accounts 6.0.0 researches companies you
supply, adds a why-now from the last 90 days and gives each company a verdict:
Contact now, Not yet or Skip. Results from 6.0.0 will replace this sample after
its verification run.

## Starting brief

- Customer: United Kingdom B2B digital-marketing agencies with 11–50 employees,
  serving SaaS companies and offering SEO or AI-search optimisation.
- Buyer roles: founder, chief executive, managing director or a senior
  strategy, growth or marketing leader.
- Requested outcome: five companies ready to contact.
- Approved maximum: **$0.500000**.
